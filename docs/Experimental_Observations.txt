GREENPULSE - EXPERIMENTAL OBSERVATIONS

EXPERIMENTAL SETUP
Dataset: Alibaba Cluster Trace 2018
Tasks: 5000
Main ablation sleep power ratio: 10%%
Power model: Linear idle-to-peak
Energy accounting: Event-driven time integration
Carbon accounting: Regional grid factors
Server locations: Simulated regional model

MAIN FINDINGS
1. Baseline energy: 16.781187 kWh
2. Baseline carbon: 13.538258 kg CO2
3. Full model energy: 12.833293 kWh
4. Full model carbon: 10.379790 kg CO2
5. Energy reduction: 23.53%%
6. Carbon reduction: 23.33%%
7. Baseline scheduled tasks: 4722
8. Full model scheduled tasks: 4723

CONSOLIDATION OBSERVATION
Models 4, 5, 8 and 9 produced identical placement and environmental results.
Consolidation scoring was verified separately through a diagnostic run.
No independent measurable consolidation benefit was observed in these experiments.

LIMITATIONS
Server locations and carbon intensities are simulated inputs.
Results are from trace-driven simulation, not physical data centers.
Sleep power is an assumed proportion of idle power.
The 5000-task dataset contains the first 5000 valid preprocessed tasks.
